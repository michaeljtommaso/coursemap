CU Coursemap: a degree planner for Cornell students

Plan every semester at Cornell on one board. Search the full Cornell course catalog, drag classes into semesters, see the prerequisites each class needs and the later classes it unlocks, and watch your progress toward your major, minors and college requirements as you plan.

  • Majors in Engineering, Arts & Sciences and CALS, with double majors and up to three minors
  • AP, IB and transfer credit counted toward your requirements
  • Class times, instructors and professor ratings for the coming semester
  • See which majors and minors the classes you like point to

Free to use, no sign-up needed. An independent student project, not an official Cornell University site.
